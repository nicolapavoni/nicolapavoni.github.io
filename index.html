About

My research is in macroeconomics, behavioural economics, public economics, the economics of information, consumption and, more recently, political economy.

My work has been published in Econometrica, the American Economic Review, the Review of Economic Studies, the Journal of Economic Theory, Theoretical Economics, the Journal of the European Economic Association, the Economic Journal and the Journal of Public Economics, among others.
